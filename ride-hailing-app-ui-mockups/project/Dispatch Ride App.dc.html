A ride app set like a newspaper: fares printed, not negotiated.
Built on the Broadsheet system, so the whole product is Source Serif on paper — no sans chrome, no boxes for layout, cyan as the only interactive ink and magenta reserved for destination and safety. The map is treated as newsprint imagery: a halftone-screened plate with the route printed in cyan and the drop pin as a magenta square.
Assumptions I made
Upfront fixed fares are the product's spine (no surge), so price is stated on every screen and reconciled on the receipt. One city, one currency, card-on-file. Serif at interface sizes needs air — I kept the 1.25× spacing and 16px body minimum, and 48px+ tap targets throughout.
